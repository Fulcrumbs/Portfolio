Old School Runescape

Why is this game the best? It's nice a and repetitive while having a satisfying gameplay loop of getting more ways to make number go up quickly.

The most 'difficult' skill

Runecrafting is an infamously slow skill to raise in runescape and often requiring lots of clicks, which is awful for the average runescape player as they usually only want to afk and NOT play the game

The most 'fun' mini-game to me

Barbarian Assault is such a fun team-based mini-game that requires all participants to co-operate. Can get toxic when your corrosponding teammate doesn't use their horn to announce your correct actions but very satisfying to complete a round

The most popular skill

Combat is the most popular activity in all of runescape, I enjoy the skills but the average fan will likely say that slayer is their favourite skill as it unlocks great money-making methods by unlocking rare enemies with expensive drops, the variety for combat is also provided to players through their slayer tasks, allowing them to participate in their favourite activity, which is combat, but against a large variety of enemies. People also paradoxially attempt to circumvent this variety by usage of their block list to try and increase their odds of getting the same high exp rate monsters over and over
